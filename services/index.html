The website

One complete website, designed around your business and built by hand.

  • Design to your direction

    You choose the look — the typefaces, the colours, the feel of it. We take those choices and turn them into a website that holds together on every page.

  • Hand-built pages

    Written by hand rather than dragged together in a builder. Nothing you have to keep paying a third party to keep switched on.

  • Mobile optimisation

    Most people will meet you on a phone. Every layout is drawn again for small screens rather than shrunk down to fit them.

  • Your services, set out clearly

    What you do, who it is for and what it costs, laid out so nobody has to ring up to find out the basics.

  • Contact information

    Phone, email, address, opening hours and a working enquiry form, so a customer can reach you whichever way suits them.

  • Fast and accessible

    Keyboard navigation, visible focus, real contrast and pages that load on a bad connection. Built in, not added on.

  • Launch

    We put it live with you on a call, and stay close afterwards while the small things surface.

Getting online

An address for the website to live at. Two ways to do it, and one of them is free.

  • A free address

    If you would rather not buy a domain, your website goes live at an address like yourbusiness.vercel.app at no cost. It works exactly the same way. This website uses one.

  • Domain name service

    We find the name, register it, and point it at your website. Domains start at £15 a year, the exact cost depends on the name, and we check and tell you before anything is bought.

  • Business email

    Up to three professional addresses on your own domain, set up on your phone and your computer. Additional addresses are £5 each.

After launch

Nothing is compulsory, and nothing renews unless you ask for it.

  • Standard updates

    New photographs, changed prices, a rewritten section, new opening hours. From £50, priced individually.

  • An update plan

    Optional. Up to five updates a month for £45 a month, for a business whose website changes often.

  • Larger work

    New pages, new sections, a second location. Quoted in writing before a minute is spent on it.

  • It stays yours

    The design, the code and the content belong to you outright. If you ever want to move it elsewhere, we hand it over without making it difficult.
