About

A small studio,
on purpose.

Sitefold is an independent web design studio. We design and build websites for businesses that either do not have one, or have one that is quietly costing them work.

No account managers
No hand-offs · No jargon

Small businesses deserve websites that feel as considered as those of much larger companies.

Fig. 02
The whole argument,
in one sentence.

The gap between what a large company's website looks like and what a small one's looks like has almost nothing to do with budget any more. It is a gap in attention.

A café with one room and a good eye should be able to have a website as carefully made as a chain with forty. That is the entire idea, and everything else here is a consequence of it.

How we work

Directly, and in plain English.

Sitefold is run by Arjun, its founder, and you deal with the person doing the work. There is no account manager between you and the design, and no stage where a project is handed to somebody who has not read the brief.

Everything is quoted before it starts. Nothing renews without you asking. If a project is not right for us, we will say so early and suggest somewhere better rather than take it on badly.

  • We do notlock work behind a monthly subscription
  • We do notuse page builders and call it custom
  • We do notpublish other people's work as if it were ours
  • We do notinvent numbers to look bigger than we are

On portfolios

Sitefold is new, so we are not going to fill a portfolio page with work we have not done. This website is the example: every page, layout, interaction and line of code on it is ours. Judge us on it. As real client work goes live, it will appear here with their name on it and their permission behind it.
