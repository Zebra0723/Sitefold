Hello

I took my first photograph at thirteen, and never really put the camera down.

It was on a trip to Michigan’s Adventure, and I knew straight away this was the thing I loved. That was more than ten years ago. I’m Robin — I have three children of my own, and I’ve been photographing families here in Genesee County for the last three years.

Most of my clients come back, which is the part I’m proudest of. My oldest is usually first in line for a photoshoot, so don’t be surprised if she tags along and ends up modelling.

About Robin

Photographs your family will actually put on the wall.

Rose Blossom Photography is just me: one photographer, working across Genesee County, doing this because I love it rather than because it scaled well.

Nobody needs to be in matching outfits and the house does not need tidying. The best photographs I take are almost always the ones nobody was posing for.
